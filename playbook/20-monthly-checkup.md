# Monthly checkup

- **Fixes:** things quietly drifting back to how they were
- **Time:** 10 minutes, once a month
- **Who:** either partner can run it; both for the last question
- **Devices:** an iPhone is enough
- **Before you start:** the three fixes are done, or far enough along that there is something to check
- **What changes:** nothing is removed. The checkup may hide a finished schedule or renew a connection, with a yes; anything bigger becomes a Checklist item
- **What stays the same:** everything else
- **Undo:** a hidden calendar is ticked back on; a renewed connection can be disconnected
- **Last checked:** 2026-11-10

## Why this matters

A tidy setup drifts. A new account gets a quick reused password, a team schedule ends and a new one starts, a promise gets made out loud and never written down. Ten minutes a month catches these while they are small. The reminder for it is the repeating "10-minute checkup" event they added to the shared calendar at the end of Fix 3.

## Before you start

Load the latest Map. Tell them this takes ten minutes and that nothing gets removed. If the repeating reminder isn't on their calendar yet, add it at the end (the last step of `12-module-commitment-capture.md` shows how).

## Parts and steps

Ask these one at a time. Anything that needs more than two minutes to fix becomes a Checklist item for another sitting.

### Part 1. The six questions (10 minutes)

#### Step 1.1 Passwords
- **Who / where:** whoever is present, with the password app open on their phone
- **Why:** the password app only protects what is inside it.
- **Do:** ask "Did either of you create a new account or change a password this month? Is it in the password app?" Then have them open the app's security check (the Map's agreement says which app; `10-module-password-manager.md` says where the check is) and tell you how many items it flags as reused or weak. They tell you the count, never the passwords.
- **Check:** the count is the same as or lower than last month's. Record it in the Map.
- **Undo:** nothing changed.

#### Step 1.2 The way back in
- **Who / where:** whoever is present; ask about both
- **Why:** a recovery plan nobody can find is not a plan.
- **Do:** ask "Do you both still know where the printed emergency sheets are?" and "Has either of you changed phone number?" A changed number means the second sign-in step on every account needs the new number added before the old one is dropped; make that a Checklist item for both.
- **Check:** they answer without hesitating. Do not ask where the sheets are.
- **Undo:** nothing changed.

#### Step 1.3 Calendars
- **Who / where:** whoever is present, on their iPhone
- **Why:** followed schedules end with a season, and stale calendars hide the real ones.
- **Do:** ask "Is anything showing on your calendar that shouldn't be, or missing that should be?" and "Has any kids' activity or club started or finished?" For one that has finished, offer to hide it now (untick it in **Calendars**) and note it in the Map. For one that has started, following it is Part 4 of `11-module-shared-calendar.md`; make it a Checklist item. Removing anything follows Part 6 of that module, never this checkup.
- **Check:** have them read out tomorrow from the shared calendars. If both are present, the two should match.
- **Undo:** tick a hidden calendar back on.

#### Step 1.4 Promises
- **Who / where:** both if possible
- **Why:** this is the habit most likely to slip.
- **Do:** ask "Since last checkup, was anything missed, or did one of you have to rescue the other?" If yes, walk back through it without blame: was it ever captured? If not, what would have made capturing it easy in that moment? Adjust the capture move or the alerts as `12-module-commitment-capture.md` describes.
- **Check:** they can each name how they captured their most recent promise.
- **Undo:** any setting adjusted here is changed back on the same screen.

#### Step 1.5 The assistant
- **Who / where:** whoever is present
- **Why:** connections expire quietly.
- **Do:** if you have a connection to their calendar, read tomorrow's events and say them back, and name what you cannot see (the Map lists it). If you can't reach the calendar at all, tell them the connection needs renewing and point them to the steps in the assistant notes file; they sign in again on the provider's own screen.
- **Check:** what you read back matches their phone.
- **Undo:** a connection can be disconnected in the assistant's settings.

#### Step 1.6 What next
- **Who / where:** both partners
- **Why:** the parked problems are still problems; this is where the next fix gets chosen.
- **Do:** read out the Map's parking lot and ask "Is any of these bothering you enough to tackle next?"
- **Check:** their answer is recorded in the Map under *Questions for the helper*, so the helper sees it.
- **Undo:** nothing changed.

**Safe stopping point.** Save the Map.

## If your screen doesn't match

There are few screens in this checkup. If the password app's security check has moved, ask them to look for words like "Watchtower", "Security", "Reports" or "Recommendations" inside the app, and follow the general routine in `00-start-here.md`.

## Undo

A calendar hidden during the checkup is ticked back on in **Calendars**. Any other fix that comes out of the checkup follows the undo steps of the module it belongs to.

## Done when

- Each of the six questions has been asked and answered.
- The Map shows this month's checkup date and the count from step 1.1.
- Any follow-up is on the Checklist with a name next to it.

## Update the Map

- Add a line to the Change log: `checkup done`, the date, and who was there.
- Set *Last checkup* in the Checklist, and record the reused-or-weak count under *Passwords*.
- Add follow-ups to the Checklist.
- Save the Map as a new version.
