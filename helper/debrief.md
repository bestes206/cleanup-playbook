# Pilot debrief

A short conversation with both partners once they have finished the first slice in the sense below, or five weeks after their first session, whichever comes first. Twenty minutes, in person or on a call. It is a conversation, not a survey: no scores.

The pilot succeeded if three things are true:

1. Both partners finished the first slice. The modules contain deliberate waiting periods, so for the debrief "finished" means: the joint session; Fix 1 through Part 6 with the first four groups of accounts done (Part 7, retiring the notes, may still be waiting out its two weeks); Fix 2 through Part 5; and Fix 3 through the one-week check. Expect this to take three to five weeks of short sittings.
2. They say things feel clearer.
3. Their assistant can reach the information they expected it to reach.

## Before the call

- Read the feedback log and the latest Map.
- Copy out the example questions they gave in the intake (Map section *The assistant*, under "What we want it to be able to answer").

## Questions

Ask both partners; their answers will differ, and the difference is the useful part.

**Did it work?**

1. "Since we started, has anything been missed or needed a rescue call?" If yes, walk through what happened: was it captured anywhere?
2. "Where do you look now to see what's on tomorrow? Is that different from before?"
3. "When did you last use the password app? What for?"
4. "Is there anything you used to carry in your head that you don't have to any more?"

**Does it feel clearer?**

5. "If a friend asked what changed, what would you tell them?"
6. "What still feels messy?"

**How was the process?**

7. "Was there a moment you wanted to quit, or did quit? What was happening?"
8. "Was there a step where you weren't sure it was safe to continue?"
9. "Did the time estimates match?"
10. "Did picking up where you left off work, including when the other one of you had done the last session?"

**The assistant test.** Do this live, with them, in their project:

11. Ask the assistant each of the example questions they gave in the intake. For each one, note: answered correctly, answered wrongly, or couldn't reach the information. A wrong answer matters more than a "can't see that".

**Next**

12. "Of the things we parked (inboxes, notifications, messages, business software), which one bothers you most now?"

## After the call

- Add what you learned to the feedback log.
- Turn each repeated problem into a playbook change.
- Update the Map's parking lot with their answer to question 12; it picks the next module to build.
