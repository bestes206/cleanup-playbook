# Feedback log

Every time the pilot couple reaches out, add a row. A reach-out is not a failure: it is the playbook telling you where it is unclear, wrong, or out of date. The log is how the next couple gets a better playbook.

Keep it free of anything sensitive, the same as the Map: no passwords, codes, account numbers or client details. Use first names or "A" and "B". If this repo may become public, keep your working copy of the log outside the repo, or use initials only.

## How to log

Log within the day, while you still remember what they actually said. Their words matter more than your diagnosis.

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Date | When they reached out |
| Who, and how | Which partner; text, call, in person |
| Where | Playbook file and step number (ask them to read you the checklist's "where we left off" line) |
| What they said | Their words, as close as you can get |
| What was actually wrong | Your read: unclear wording, wrong step, screen changed, assistant went off-script, missing case, fear of breaking something, lost progress |
| How it got unstuck | What you told them or did |
| Minutes of your time | Roughly |
| Playbook change | The edit that would have prevented the call, or "none: one-off" |

## Categories

Tag each entry with one, so patterns show up after a handful:

- `wording`: they didn't understand what the assistant said
- `screen`: what they saw didn't match the step
- `assistant`: the assistant skipped a check, asked several questions at once, forgot the Map, or made something up
- `state`: progress or the Map was lost, stale, or not visible to the other partner
- `fear`: they stopped because they were worried about breaking something
- `gap`: their situation isn't covered
- `product`: a tool (password manager, calendar, connector) didn't behave as described
- `other`

## Also worth noting (no reach-out needed)

- Sessions that ended early, and at which step.
- Anything the assistant did that you would not have done.
- Steps that took much longer than the estimate.
